The game

Videos freeze at a chosen moment; after you pick, the result is shown. Points and XP are computed by server-side game rules. A correct guess earns points and a wrong guess loses points; points reset every week. The same video cannot earn points twice. Duels are five questions that two players can each complete at their own time.

Lives: each day you get a set number of lives (currently 5). A wrong guess uses one life; when you run out you cannot make new guesses that day, and your lives are restored the next day. Unused lives do not carry over, and duel rounds do not use lives. The number of lives may change.

Points, XP, badges, and rankings have no monetary value; they cannot be cashed out or used for betting. There are no cash prizes and no betting. Points are not sold.

Clipra Plus and purchases

Clipra Plus is an optional paid membership that gives you unlimited lives. The monthly and yearly options are auto-renewing subscriptions; the lifetime option is a one-time purchase, is not a subscription, and never renews. The price is shown in the app and on the App Store before you buy.

Payment is charged to your Apple account when you confirm the purchase. A subscription renews automatically for the same period, and your Apple account is charged, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage and cancel your subscription in Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions; cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period. Refund requests are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com). Deleting your Clipra account does not cancel your subscription.

A purchase is associated with your Clipra account. If you change devices, you can use "Restore purchases" with the same Apple account.
