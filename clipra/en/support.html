Support

For help with Clipra, a content correction, or a rights-infringement report, you can write to cepikkurtnoyan@icloud.com. Operator: Noyan Çepikkurt.

You can also send a report from inside the app: the ⋯ menu on the leaderboard/duel screens for users, the source screen for videos.

Reporting a video or question

Send the link from the video's source screen, the question, and a short description of the issue. Never share your password, sign-in code, or private account key.

Account and privacy

You can reset your password from the sign-in screen, and close your account via Settings → Delete Account. If you can't access the app, you can ask support for help; we may need to verify the account is yours.
